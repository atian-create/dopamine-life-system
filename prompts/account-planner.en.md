# Creator account-planner prompt

Use my supplied colorful page as the visual reference. Make a page that clearly explains the distinct purpose of each creator account.

My material:
- Who I am and what I want to do: [fill in]
- Accounts and platforms: [fill in]
- Existing content, audience and purpose of each account: [fill in]
- Available time and production capacity: [fill in]
- Creations, methods or products I want to share: [fill in]
- Information that must remain private: [fill in]

Start with a role map, then make an account card for each account. Include a one-sentence positioning statement, audience, purpose, three recurring series with suggested topics, production format and exclusions. End with different angles on a shared theme and a weekly schedule that fits my capacity.

Do not add accounts merely to fill the layout. Do not treat sample followers, revenue, commercial arrangements or someone else's experience as my facts. Omit unavailable metrics and label suggested topics as suggestions. Check current platform requirements for publishing rules.

Match the reference: saturated colors, bold black outlines, hard shadows, rounded cards, sticker labels, illustrated characters and large headings. Make series, boundaries and scheduling easy to scan; allow wide tables to scroll on mobile.

Deliver ready-to-open Chinese and English pages with equivalent content, actual screenshots and customization instructions.
