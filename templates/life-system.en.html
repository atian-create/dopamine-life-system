04

My boundaries

Use your own criteria before turning every opportunity into a task.

Time

Keep some unscheduled time

Check whether a last-minute task interrupts rest.

→ Agree on a clear deadline.

Scope

One focus for this season

Put new ideas on a list to try later.

→ Decide at your next review.

Collaboration

Clarify deliverables and ownership

Define what you own and what needs help.

→ Align with a one-page brief.

05

What I am working on

Put life, creative work and projects on the same map.

Life

One city walk each week

Record streets, food and people you meet.

→ One page of walking notes each week.

Creative work

Make a weekend mini-zine

Combine photos and notes into a shareable page.

→ Start with a four-page version.

Project

Turn experience into a template

Use examples you have actually tried.

→ Ask one friend to try it.

06

Where I get stuck

Turn each obstacle into an adjustable action. Start with one.

Starting

Waiting for a perfect version

Finish one page you can open.

→ Make only the first page today.

Switching

Too many ideas split my attention

Capture ideas, then choose once a week.

→ Keep one active piece of work.

Reviewing

Forgetting to document the result

Save a screenshot and three sentences when you finish.

→ Record what you made, learned and will change.

07

My operating rhythm

Make a plan you can follow and adjust.

Daily

Finish one small thing

Choose one action and make time for it.

→ Write one discovery in the evening.

Weekly

Keep a weekly page

Notice what gives energy and what needs less time.

→ Choose one focus for next week.

Monthly

Revisit direction

Decide what to continue and what to pause.

→ Update this life-system page.

08

What I could try next

Choose one based on interest and capacity. No need to start everything.

Green light

Make a walking map

Reuse your photos and route; share with a friend.

→ Finishable in one weekend.

Amber light

Try a small talk

Talk about one project you actually made.

→ Check preparation time before committing.

Red light

Start three projects at once

It may fragment your time.

→ Keep it on the later list.
